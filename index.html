Questions

Common questions

How do I book a car?

Open Our Fleet, pick a car and press Book car. Fill in the form, check the bill and confirm.

Can I pick the car up myself?

Yes. Choose Self-Drive and then pick-up from the dealership. There is no delivery charge.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery, or a chauffeur-driven rental, adds $50 to your bill.

How do I pay?

Pay the amount on your bill, then attach a screenshot of the payment in the booking form.
